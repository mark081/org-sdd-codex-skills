# Organizational boundary knowledge

Apply this reference only to explicitly participating repositories. Keep knowledge in the existing `.sdd/knowledge/` bundle; do not create a central graph or another local task-status store.

## Establish the supplied baseline

Discover `coordinate-org-sdd` through the installed skill catalog and read its `SKILL.md`, record/reference contracts and the selected operation guidance. Use its CLI contract for commands and readiness scopes. An absent local pointer and no session participation preserve standalone analysis. Malformed participation, a mismatched initiative digest, a missing installed skill or inaccessible authorized coordination snapshot blocks affected organizational claims; report the missing input and continue independently supported local analysis. Provide a manual handoff when tooling or authorized evidence is missing. Do not assume adjacent source-repository skill paths, install tooling, fetch a locator or modify another repository.

Resolve the supplied initiative, repository and handoff IDs against the authorized coordination workspace. Preserve the pinned initiative/handoff/contract identities and policy baseline. Run organizational structure and relevant readiness checks separately from the local brownfield validator; structural validity does not establish approval. Follow the coordination reference rules for full Git revisions and raw-byte source digests, canonical record digests and explicitly uncommitted snapshots. An inaccessible source keeps its supplied identity; never substitute a guessed hash or claim remote HEAD currency.

## Curate into relevant local concepts

Add boundary knowledge to the relevant interface, component or dependency concept; use `open-questions.md` for missing authority, inaccessible evidence and coverage gaps. Keep the existing OKF source, generated, revision, fingerprint and curation fields. Unknown producer extensions and human-confirmed content survive reconciliation.

For each material boundary claim, record in the concept body:

| Information | Required provenance and distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Ownership | Repository/contract/handoff identity, supplied owner and role or unresolved decision ID, and the observed source record identity/digest under the snapshot rule below. Repository access or graph clustering never assigns authority. |
| Shared obligation | Initiative ID and direct contract ID/digest, provider and affected consumers, authoritative definition SourceRefs and recorded review/approval scope. Display version is descriptive, not compatibility evidence. |
| External dependency | Handoff/dependency IDs, supplying/receiving owners, required producer stage and consumer stage, obligation pins and evidence references. Contract approval and local completion remain different prerequisites. |
| Claim quality | Observation time, source basis/revision/digest, extracted/inferred/ambiguous classification where applicable, current/stale/conflicted/unresolved disposition and actual review status. A draft remains unreviewed. |
| Missing input | Inaccessible identity or unresolved decision, affected concept/handoff/gate, responsible supplied role (or explicitly unassigned), next owner action and independently supported work. |

Avoid reciprocal content hashes. If an initiative, handoff, knowledge record or other source record directly or transitively pins this OKF payload, any digest embedded back into the OKF identifies a preserved **observed snapshot**, with its observation time and resolvable historical bytes; it is not a demand for that record's final current digest. Verify current participation and readiness separately through the supplied participation pointer and coordination records. Direct contract pins identify the current reviewed obligation unless the contract itself reciprocally depends on this content; in that case preserve an observed snapshot and reconcile the current obligation separately. Never chase final reciprocal hashes or fabricate a digest to close a cycle.

Use normal OKF `sources` links for evidence discovery and preserve exact organizational references in the body or existing producer metadata; do not invent a second approval schema. Keep source/configuration evidence authoritative for implemented behavior, and owner-approved contracts authoritative for agreed obligations. When they disagree, preserve both baselines and request owner review. Graphify can suggest a relationship to investigate; it cannot approve an obligation or resolve a conflict. Do not copy private source/OKF content into organizational records. Authorized references can remain unresolved; a redacted attestation is sufficient only when adopter policy explicitly accepts its scope.

The local bundle validator checks the existing producer contract, fingerprints and links. It does not resolve organizational pins, authenticate owners, validate external approvals or establish synchronization. Initial baselines and material changes to human-confirmed concepts retain the existing human review stop even when both validators pass.

## Refresh and outgoing update

Keep Update Mode's ordering: refresh Graphify where required; capture `--diff-manifest` before changing the manifest or source metadata; map changed paths and record concept dispositions; only then write the replacement manifest, update source metadata and validate. Retain the pre-update diff as evidence. Graph refresh alone does not reconcile a concept.

Also compare externally supplied record/source/policy pins with the last observed boundary references. External contract or ownership changes can invalidate knowledge while the local source fingerprint remains equal. Preserve old pins and historical approval records, identify affected consumers and handoffs, and propose deliberate reconciled pins with compatibility or migration review where needed. Do not repin merely to obtain a passing check. Mark affected concepts stale/conflicted until reconciled; inaccessible private context remains unresolved.

After reconciliation, prepare an outgoing update using the installed coordination skill's knowledge/evidence templates and exact schemas, only within the authorized workspace. Include authorized source and OKF reference identities, contract pins, affected participants, freshness, observation/review state, changed-reference impact and the local pre-update diff/validation evidence references. Keep private content local. If no authorized outgoing workspace or disclosure decision is available, return the intended destination, reference inventory and missing decision as a manual handoff instead of copying records externally.

New outgoing records remain draft/unreviewed and publication remains `pending` until actual review and publication evidence is supplied. `not_required` requires the exact applicable resolved adopter decision; `published` requires matching payload/evidence identities. Evidence pins exported source/OKF payloads, never the knowledge record containing its own evidence ID. A local file, draft PR or validator report does not establish publication.

Return the analyzed revision/worktree, old/new source fingerprints and changed boundary pins, concept dispositions, exact local and organizational checks, unresolved private inputs, required human review, and publication disposition. Verified local implementation can coexist with a pending outgoing update; the affected handoff is not current until its configured review and publication conditions pass. Drafting does not dispatch work, publish, grant release authority or edit local `TASKS.md`.
